## After install

<Steps>

1. **Say SE or MP**  
   Pick one Helidon 4 programming model and stick to it.

2. **Point at a module or paste a class**  
   Give the Bot a concrete starting point in your workspace.

3. **For agents: ask “design the MCP surface” before generating a server**  
   Align the tool surface before scaffolding.

</Steps>
